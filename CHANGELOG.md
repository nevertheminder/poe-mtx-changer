# Changelog

## 0.5.2 (2026-11-21)

- The window title no longer says "(Prototype)".
- The header artwork fills the whole width on windows wider than 1536 px.
- Release builds no longer contain build paths from the developer's PC.

## 0.5.1 (2026-11-21)

First public release.

- **Catalog** of the shop cosmetics in your game, with names, icons and the shop's categories.
- **Apply and restore**: changes are checked before they are written, a backup is made first, and anything that goes
  wrong is rolled back. **Restore originals** puts the game back as it was.
- **3D wardrobe**: your set on your character with the game's own effects, glow, weapon outlines and moving capes and
  ribbons (switch off with **Cloth physics**). Smooth animation, idle and run, several lighting modes.
- **Skill effects and auras** for your gems, with shop names and icons.
- **Sound** replacements and **profiles** for several sets.
- Hold **Alt** over an icon to see it bigger; **×** on a wardrobe tile clears it.
- Oodle is found automatically if it is on your PC; otherwise the app explains where to get it.
- Own logo and header art.
